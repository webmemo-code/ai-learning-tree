Form moodboard — twelve structural ideas, one real dataset

Every sketch draws the same data/growth-log.jsonl: 19 monthly buckets, 8 live sectors (2 are permanently empty), size = commits only, mix = texture. The three 3D variants failed because the tree was a chart wearing bark — geometry placed at computed coordinates instead of grown. These twelve try to make the form itself carry data. Reading the marks. Vertical = time, oldest at the bottom, always monthly. Width/area = commits in that bucket. Hue = sector (taxonomy default-v1). Composition (stripes, dots, roughness, dashes) = the commit/pr/review/issue mix.
The three facts every sketch must not hide: 2025-02 is absent from the log entirely (a real gap, drawn as a gap); 2025-05 has exactly one commit; build.no-code and build.low-code have never had a single event.
