Angular asymmetry — 1.00 = all commits in one compass direction, 0 = spread evenly. Hollow dots are months with <10 commits, where the number is arithmetic noise rather than a habit.
sqrt scale on radius: 155 commits is ~12x the radius of 1, not 155x.
Core radius is a drawn floor, not data — every zero rests on it.
2025-02 is missing from the log entirely: a real pinch, never interpolated.
8 sectors, not 10. no-code and low-code are dropped from this page: work that avoids the dev setup never produces a commit, so a commit-derived view cannot observe them at all.
Bearings are respaced, 45° apart, in taxonomy order — presentation only. Dropping two sectors left a 125° void; an even octagon gives every lobe the same angular budget. Azimuth here means “which sector”, not a compass direction, and generator/taxonomy.mjs is unchanged.
Rosettes are the measurement. Their outlines and lobe dots sit on true radii at true month heights. The skin between two rosettes is Catmull-Rom inference — which is why the skin is the faint thing and the rosettes glow.
