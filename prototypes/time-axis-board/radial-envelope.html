Radial envelope

Monthly activity as a lofted 3D surface. Radius at (month, bearing) = that sector's commits that month.

skin commit/pr/review/issue mix (colour + banding, never size)
rosettes the 19 cross-sections at their TRUE radii, inside the glass
base spiky & lopsided → top rounder = the finding

Alpha 0 = rosettes alone. Bloom is multiplicative on alpha: raise one, lower the other, or the eight sector hues wash to a single white. Current defaults are deliberately hot (.17 / .94 / .32) — pull bloom back toward .62 if the hues stop telling apart.
Commits per sector per month
Angular asymmetry — 1.00 = all commits in one compass direction, 0 = spread evenly. Hollow dots are months with <10 commits, where the number is arithmetic noise rather than a habit.
sqrt scale on radius: 155 commits is ~12x the radius of 1, not 155x.
Core radius is a drawn floor, not data — every zero rests on it.
2025-02 is missing from the log entirely: a real pinch, never interpolated.
8 sectors, not 10. no-code and low-code are dropped from this page: work that avoids the dev setup never produces a commit, so a commit-derived view cannot observe them at all. Their bearings (270°, 310°) are left empty, which is why one flank is a long smooth sweep.
Rosettes are the measurement. Their outlines and lobe dots sit on true radii at true month heights. The skin between two rosettes is Catmull-Rom inference — which is why the skin is the faint thing and the rosettes glow.
